Both Champion cross country teams finish top 10 at state

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 8, 2026

Boerne Champion closed out the 2026 cross country season Saturday morning at the 5A state meet at Old Settlers Park in Round Rock, with both teams finishing in the top 10.

The Lady Chargers were sixth with 177 points, beating former district foe Smithson Valley by four points after the Rangers finished seventh at 181. New Braunfels won the 5A girls title.

The Champion boys were eighth with 200 points. Austin LASA won the boys title, and Weslaco East, the Region IV-5A champion, placed second.

The boys will graduate six of their seven state runners.
