Commissioners Court honors the late Judge Frieda Pressler

Kendall County Commissioners Court paused its Tuesday session to honor the late Judge Frieda Pressler, presenting a memorial plaque to her family in the courtroom.

Commissioner Richard Chapman, presiding as judge pro tem, made the presentation at the county judge's request. The plaque reads, in part: "In loving memory of Judge Frieda Pressler, dedicated public servant, leader, colleague, friend. Thank you for a lifetime of service to Kendall County."

"If I were ever going to speak against term limits, I would take a large picture of Frieda Pressler, and I would take her record, and put it in front of these people," Chapman said. "This lady did such a wonderful job for over 40 years."

Pressler's service to Kendall County began with Kendall County EMS in the early 1980s. In 1986 she took office as Precinct 4 justice of the peace, a position she held until her death on September 6, 2026, at the age of 70. In May 2024, Commissioners Court recognized her for 40 years of county service.

Family members accepted the plaque and thanked the court, and the two women who worked alongside her, for supporting and taking care of her over the years.
