Court sets December 8 interviews for Precinct 4 constable, raises retirement contribution

Kendall County Commissioners Court set the process Tuesday for appointing a Precinct 4 constable to serve the remainder of Brian Vaughn's unexpired term, until the next general election.

Under the guidelines the court approved 4 to 0, interested parties submit letters of interest to the county human resources department by 5 PM on December 4. Qualifications will be verified by December 7, and the court will interview candidates at its December 8 meeting, after the regular court business is finished. The appointment takes effect January 1, 2027. The court is following the same format it used for the Precinct 4 justice of the peace appointment earlier this year: candidates are sequestered, and each member of the court brings questions.

In other action Tuesday, with the county judge absent and Commissioner Richard Chapman presiding as judge pro tem:

Retirement contribution. The court voted 3 to 1 to raise the county's employer contribution to the Texas County and District Retirement System from 195 percent to 200 percent, effective January 1, 2027. Commissioner Jennifer McCall voted against. The increase was approved in the county budget; Tuesday's order is the formal action the retirement system requires, due to the system by December 15.

Library agreements. The court voted 4 to 0 to extend the Comfort Public Library agreement through fiscal year 2027, with quarterly payments rising from 45,240 dollars to 46,371 dollars, an annual total of 185,484 dollars. The court also voted 4 to 0 to continue the agreement with the City of Boerne for library privileges at the Patrick Heath Public Library through fiscal year 2027, with quarterly payments rising from 78,047 dollars to 79,998.25 dollars, an annual total of 319,993 dollars.

EMS medical director. The court voted 4 to 0 to approve an agreement with Dr. Mark T. Ogden for EMS medical direction services, at 7,500 dollars per month, running from November 24, 2026, to September 30, 2027, with possible renewals, and approved the related HIPAA business associate agreement.

Travel. The court voted 4 to 0 to approve out-of-state travel for development engineer Clay Forrester to attend the My Government Online user conference in New Orleans, December 9 through 11. The court voted 3 to 1, Chapman against, to authorize out-of-state travel for Fire Marshal's Office employees to attend the New Fire and Arson Investigator Academy in Las Vegas, December 7 through 11.

No action. The court took no action on a proposed vehicle purchase for the Precinct 4 justice of the peace, after discussion of vehicle options and costs.

Dog park presentation. The Kendall County Partnership for Parks presented proposed enhancements to the dog park at Joshua Springs Park and Preserve, to be pursued with potential grant funding. The presenters told the court the park drew 175,000 visitors in the last 12 months. The item was a presentation; no action was taken.
