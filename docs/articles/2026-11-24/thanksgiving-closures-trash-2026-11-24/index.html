County courthouse and Boerne City Hall close November 26 and 27; trash collection slides one day

The Kendall County Courthouse will be closed Thursday, November 26, and Friday, November 27, in observance of the Thanksgiving holiday, the courthouse announced in a November 23 Facebook post. The Kendall County Health Department building will also be closed November 26 and 27, per the department.

Boerne City Hall, including Boerne Utilities, will be closed Thursday, November 26, and Friday, November 27, for Thanksgiving, per the City of Boerne holiday notice dated November 19.

Trash and recycling collection for Boerne Utilities customers with service provided by WM will be delayed by one day for Thanksgiving, per the city notice posted on the City of Boerne Waste Collection page. There will be no collections on Thanksgiving, Thursday, November 26. Customers with a regular Thursday collection day will be collected Friday, November 27, and customers with a regular Friday collection day will be collected Saturday, November 28.

The city notice lists the same one-day delay for Christmas and New Year's Day. Questions go to Boerne Utilities Customer Care and Billing at 830-249-9511, option 1, per the notice.

What happens next: County and city offices reopen after the holiday weekend, and Boerne collection returns to its regular schedule the following week.
