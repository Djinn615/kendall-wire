Theatre Director Crowley Steps Down December 31

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 31, 2026

Christine Crowley steps down as executive director of the Boerne Community Theatre on December 31, after nine years in the role and 25 years with the theatre, the Boerne Star reported October 30.

Crowley will continue to act as an ambassador for the venue and manage the box office, the Star reported.

Interviews for the next executive director were conducted in October, and the executive board will announce the future executive director in November, the Star reported. A retirement gathering for Crowley will be held sometime in December.

The theatre is at 907 E. Blanco Road in Boerne. Its current production, Ken Ludwig's "Moriarty," runs weekends through November 15, according to the Star calendar of events. Tickets are 20 to 24 dollars at the box office, by calling 830-249-9166, or online at boernetheatre.org.

What happens next: The board announces the next executive director in November, and the retirement gathering is held in December.
