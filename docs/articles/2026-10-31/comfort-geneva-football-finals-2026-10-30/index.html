Comfort And Geneva Win Friday Night Finals

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 31, 2026

Comfort beat Young Men's Leadership Academy 73-6 at home on Friday, October 30, and Geneva School of Boerne won 45-21 at St. Francis Episcopal in Houston, the Boerne Star reported October 30.

Comfort posted its most lopsided win of the season, the Star reported. The Bobcats led 36-0 in the first quarter and 43-0 at halftime, added 22 points in the third quarter, and won the fourth quarter 8-6. Comfort has won two straight and improved to 2-3 in district and 3-6 overall.

Geneva led St. Francis Episcopal 45-7 before St. Francis scored the final two touchdowns, the Star reported. Geneva closed the regular season 9-1 overall and 5-1 in district, locked up the No. 2 seed in its district, and opens TAPPS playoff play next week against a Dallas-area team, per the Star.

What happens next: Comfort closes the regular season Friday at Blanco. The Star reported Comfort will likely need to win that game by 18 points to give itself a chance at a playoff spot, depending on results elsewhere in district play.
