Boerne ISD teachers receive more than $200,000 in innovation grants

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 20, 2026

Boerne ISD teachers received more than $200,000 in Teacher Innovation Grants when the Boerne Education Foundation Prize Parade visited classrooms across the district, the district announced on Thursday, November 19.

The grants are awarded annually ahead of Thanksgiving and reached teachers at every Boerne ISD campus, per the district announcement. The foundation is the Boerne Education Foundation, known as BEF.

The grants fund classroom projects aligned to state standards and are intended to support critical thinking, communication, collaboration, and creativity, per the district. Each application is reviewed by a committee of BEF board members, and the grants can pay for hands-on resources, specialized equipment, and instructional programs, per the district.

At Boerne Middle School North alone, BEF awarded more than $18,500 to staff and faculty in this round, per the school newsletter dated November 19.

"At Boerne ISD, we are deeply grateful for the unwavering generosity of the Boerne Education Foundation and our community," Superintendent Dr. Kristin Craft said in the district announcement.
