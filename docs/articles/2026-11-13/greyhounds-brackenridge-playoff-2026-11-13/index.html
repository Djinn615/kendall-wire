Boerne beats Brackenridge 56-14 in playoff opener

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 13, 2026

Boerne High beat San Antonio Brackenridge 56-14 on Thursday night, November 12, at Boerne ISD Stadium in the first round of the Class 5A Division II playoffs, the Boerne Star reported.

The Greyhounds scored 28 points in the first quarter and led 35-7 at halftime. Boerne improved to 11-0 with the win. MaxPreps and Dave Campbell's Texas Football also list the final as Boerne 56, Brackenridge 14.

Two of the first-quarter touchdowns came on passes from players who do not play quarterback. Wide receiver Jacob Hicks took the ball on a reverse and threw a touchdown pass to Beau Dayoc, and lineman Jonny Waters threw a touchdown pass to Kole Traylor, the Star reported. Waters also scored a touchdown in a playoff game last year on a hook and ladder play.

Each team scored one touchdown in the second quarter. In the third quarter, Josiah Brown scored two touchdowns for Boerne and Logan Kolle ran for another, pushing the lead to 56-7.

Brackenridge scored the only touchdown of the fourth quarter, a 2-yard run by Cruz Castillo. Castillo scored both touchdowns for the Eagles, the Star reported.

Five Boerne players threw a touchdown pass in the game: Grant Sweeney, Jonny Waters, Jacob Hicks, Taylor Eamello and Cooper Hoegemeyer, according to the Star report.

What happens next: Boerne plays in the area round next week against the winner of the Mission Veterans Memorial and Mercedes game, which is played Friday night, November 13. Boerne Champion also plays Friday night, hosting Eagle Pass Winn at 7 p.m. at Boerne ISD Stadium in the Class 5A Division I first round.
