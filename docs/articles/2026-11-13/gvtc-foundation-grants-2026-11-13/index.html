GVTC Foundation awards grants to 183 nonprofits

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 13, 2026

The GVTC Foundation awarded $662,754 in Texas Community Grants to 183 nonprofit organizations in 2026, the Boerne Star reported on November 12.

The grants were awarded in two funding cycles during the year, in amounts from $500 to $15,000. More than 100 organizations accepted awards on Tuesday, November 10, at the foundation's Fall Giving Breakfast Celebration, which marked the event's 20th year.

The foundation sorts its giving into five areas. Health and Human Services received $415,300 of the 2026 total, Youth Programs and Services received $117,654, Education received $81,300, First Responders and Emergency Services received $29,000, and Cultural Programs received $19,500, according to the figures reported by the Star. The five amounts add to the $662,754 total.

"GVTC is deeply rooted in this community, and we believe real impact goes beyond providing services or writing a check," Sonia Aguillon, GVTC Foundation president, said, as quoted by the Star. "It means being present, listening and understanding what our communities are facing."

More than 87 percent of GVTC Communications employees donate to the fund, and GVTC matches those donations at 150 percent, the Star reported. Since the program began, $7.8 million has been given to area nonprofits.

Bryce Boddie, executive director of Hill Country Family Services, spoke at the breakfast. Hill Country Family Services has served Kendall County families and individuals in crisis for more than 40 years, Boddie said, as quoted by the Star.

The GVTC Foundation is the charitable arm of GVTC Communications. Its site states that it serves residents in 11 counties, including Kendall County, and that nonprofits in its five focus areas can apply for grants. The foundation also sponsors Thanksgiving Give Back, which delivers meals to families for the holiday.

What happens next: the foundation takes grant applications in two cycles each year. Application periods and guidelines are posted on the GVTC Foundation website.
