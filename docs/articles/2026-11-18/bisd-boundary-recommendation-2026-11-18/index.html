Boerne ISD trustees review middle school boundary recommendation

Boerne ISD trustees reviewed a recommendation on Monday, November 16, that would move the Esperanza neighborhood from the Boerne Middle School North attendance zone to the Voss Middle School zone, and place the full Fabra Elementary School zone in the North zone. Associate Superintendent John O'Hare led the review of the middle school attendance boundary adjustments during the board meeting, per the Boerne Star report of the meeting.

The recommendation comes from the district Campus Boundary Committee, a committee of parents, community members and staff formed in January 2026. The district's committee page states that Boerne Middle School North has been near capacity for the last two years and has been closed to transfers during that time, and that the district demographics report shows Voss Middle School has space to accommodate additional students. The committee is advisory and holds no statutory authority, per the district.

Under the recommendation, the Esperanza neighborhood would move from the North zone to the Voss zone. The second adjustment addresses the Fabra Elementary split: 88 percent of Fabra students are currently zoned to Boerne Middle School North and 12 percent to Boerne Middle School South, per the district. The recommendation moves all of the Fabra zone into the North zone.

O'Hare told trustees that Boerne Middle School North, at 240 Johns Road, has a capacity of 1,000 students that the district treats as a hard cap, because the campus has no place to add temporary buildings for additional classroom space, per the Star. Current North enrollment of 911 students is projected to reach 1,009 in 2028-29, 1,029 in 2029-30 and 1,031 in 2030-31, per the presentation. Voss Middle School currently enrolls 779 students, 65 percent of its 1,200 student capacity, per the presentation. With the adjustments, Voss enrollment is projected at 920 students in 2027-28, 930 in 2028-29 and 943 in 2029-30, per the presentation.

The committee reviewed four options, studied enrollment projections through 2036, and considered traffic flow, travel times and future development, per the Star. Voss Middle School, at 45 Voss Parkway, is 8.2 miles from the intersection of FM 46 and Esperanza Boulevard, while students in Esperanza currently travel about three miles to Boerne Middle School North, per the Star. The committee suggested extending grandfathering practices, a rule that lets students stay at their current campus, so current sixth and seventh graders could remain at their current school, per the Star.

The district took community feedback on the recommendation through a survey that closed on November 4, and the feedback was shared with trustees, per the district.

What happens next: trustees are scheduled to decide on the recommendation at their December 14 board meeting. If approved, the two adjustments take effect for the 2027-28 school year, per the Star.
