No Hallmark Movie For Boerne

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 28, 2026

Boerne will not be Hallmark's Christmas town.

The city was one of 25 nationwide finalists for the network's America's Merriest Christmas Town, but the title belongs to Carlinville, Illinois, the Boerne Star reported October 28. As its prize, Carlinville will be the backdrop of a 2027 Hallmark Christmas movie.

Hallmark received 1,500 city submissions, and Boerne was the only town in Texas selected to be considered for the grand prize, according to the network. Hallmark executives chose the 25 finalists and opened voting to the public for three weeks leading up to a September 4 cutoff. A combination of fan votes and network executives led to Carlinville being named the winner, according to a news release.

On October 15, Hallmark Channel stars Jonathan Bennett, Erin Cahill, Tamera Mowry-Housley and Brendan Penny delivered the news in person at the county courthouse in Carlinville with a surprise Christmas-in-October celebration, according to Secret Chicago, as cited by the Star.

Danielle Mullin, senior vice president of brand marketing at Hallmark Media, said in a statement that the winning submission beautifully described the camaraderie and heart of both the town and its people.

Carlinville, a town of about 6,000, is in Macoupin County about 40 miles from Springfield, Illinois, and an hour north of St. Louis.

Boerne still has screen time ahead. Bravo plans to air a reality show titled Small Town Trouble: Texas, scheduled to premiere with a two-hour episode November 18 at 8 p.m., with weekly episodes streaming the next day on Peacock.

What happens next: the Bravo premiere is November 18, and the Hallmark movie filmed in Carlinville is set for 2027.
