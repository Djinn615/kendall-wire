Early Voting Day Nine: Tuesday Draws 1,528 Voters

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 28, 2026

Day nine of early voting is in the books for Kendall County. Tuesday, October 27 drew 1,528 in-person voters, up 28 from Monday's 1,500.

The nine-day in-person total is 14,614, which the county puts at 35.38 percent turnout. The county's in-person table uses 41,302 registered voters; the county elections page lists 41,263.

Mail-ballot totals moved as well. Accepted mail ballots now total 838, up 27 from Monday's 811. The county reports 1,363 mail ballots sent, 862 received, 14 pending, and 7 undeliverable.

Combined in-person plus accepted mail ballots: 15,452, about 37.4 percent of the 41,263 registered voters listed on the county elections page.

A same-day comparison to November 2025 early voting was not included because an equivalent-day figure could not be verified from a county or Secretary of State source.

Early voting at the Courthouse Annex in Boerne runs 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. through Friday, October 30.

What happens next: early voting continues through Friday, October 30, and Election Day is Tuesday, November 3.
