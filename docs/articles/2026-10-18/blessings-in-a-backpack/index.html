500 weekend food bags: Boerne volunteers feed students facing food insecurity

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 18, 2026

Every Thursday, Boerne volunteers form an assembly line and pack hundreds of weekend food bags for students facing food insecurity.

Through the local chapter of Blessings in a Backpack, they reach more than 500 Boerne ISD students on free or reduced-price lunch, about one in five district students, across all 14 campuses. Each bag holds two breakfasts, two lunches, and several snacks to carry a student through the weekend.

Organizer Kathy Bandujo started the local chapter 14 years ago with a single school. It has grown every year since.

Every donated dollar goes to food. Bandujo and her coordinator cover paperwork and printing costs out of pocket. Rising grocery prices are straining the budget: a case of mandarins that once cost about 16 dollars now runs about 25.

Because families' privacy is protected, the volunteers never hear a thank you. They say that was never the point.
