City closes Main Street for Dickens on Main

Main Street in downtown Boerne will close to vehicles from 4 p.m. to 10 p.m. on Friday, November 27, and Saturday, November 28, for Dickens on Main.

The closure covers Main Street between Blanco Road and River Road, and roads around Main Plaza, per the Dickens on Main travel impacts map the City of Boerne posted on November 26. The Boerne Police Department and Visit Boerne posted the same map.

Dickens on Main runs along the Hill Country Mile on Friday and Saturday. In a City of Boerne video posted November 26, city representative Chris Shadrock said parking is available at City Hall and the Patrick Heath Public Library.

Drivers who do not plan to attend should use another route through downtown during the closure hours on both evenings.
