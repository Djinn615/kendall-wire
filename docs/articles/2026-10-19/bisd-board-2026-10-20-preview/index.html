Boerne ISD board to hold FIRST hearing, policy first reading at Tuesday meeting

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 19, 2026

Boerne ISD board meets Tuesday with FIRST hearing, policy first reading, and closed session on real property

The Boerne ISD Board of Trustees holds its regular monthly meeting Tuesday, October 20, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. in the BISD Board Room at 235 Johns Road, Boerne. The posted agenda runs eleven sections, from closed session through consent items, governance, reports, and business actions.

Trustees will open with a closed session covering personnel matters (Texas Government Code 551.074), personally identifiable student information (551.0821), and discussion of the purchase, exchange, lease, or value of real property (551.072). Any action on those closed-session discussions would come afterward in open session, where the board is also scheduled to consider routine personnel items.

The consent agenda is broad this month: approval of prior meeting minutes, the purchasing report, the quarterly investment report, the 2026-27 broker dealer resolution, the annual investment policy review, a first reading of board policy FNA(LOCAL), 2026-2027 campus and district performance objectives, a student intern agreement with the UT Health Science Center San Antonio, and a memorandum of understanding with Sam Houston State University for advanced certification programs.

In governance, the board will hear member reports and take up a resolution for mandatory background checks for board trustees and candidates. Reports include district updates from the superintendent, beginning-of-year elementary progress monitoring and early childhood programs, an advanced academics program report, and an attendance report from administrative services.

Business actions include a public hearing on the district's FIRST rating, the Financial Integrity Rating System of Texas financial accountability review, followed by the monthly financial report and board action on financial matters, including budget amendments, investments, donations, and tax collection reports. The meeting closes with future business items and adjournment.
