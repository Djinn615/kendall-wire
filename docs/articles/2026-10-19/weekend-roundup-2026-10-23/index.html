This Weekend in Boerne and Kendall County: October 23-25

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 19, 2026

Here is what is on around Boerne and Kendall County from Friday, October 23 to Sunday, October 25.

Friday, October 23

  • 5th Annual Boerne Chamber Pickleball Tournament presented by Alliance Credit Union (12:00 PM - 5:00 PM CDT, Fair Oaks Ranch Golf & Country Club).
  • Singer/Songwriter Live Music on the Patio @ Tapatio Springs (12:00 AM - 11:59 PM CDT).

Saturday, October 24

  • 2026 Boots and Blessings Gala Online Auction & Raffle Supporting The Hope Center Food Pantry and Outreach.
  • An Evening of Hope - A Little Black Dress Event (6:00 PM - 9:00 PM CDT, CROSS).
  • Bluegrass Jam. Bring your instrument and play along or just come to listen to the Bluegrass Jams while exploring the exhibits and grounds of The AgriCultural, 102 City Park Road, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. Free to the public. Donations are welcome.
  • Boerne Boo. For a ghastly good time, be at the Patrick Heath Public Library, 451 N. Main St., from 4-7 p.m. This free family-friendly event has arts and crafts, trick-or-treating, bounce houses, carnival games, food trucks, music and of course lots of candy and a costume contest.
  • Butterfly Day. Stop by during the Farmers Market at 33 Herff Road, for family-friendly activities from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m., including butterfly tagging and OE testing, temporary tattoos and face painting, native wildflower seedballs, and more.
  • Cibolo Farmers Market at Herff Farm (9:00 AM - 1:00 PM CDT, HERFF FARM).
  • Community Rag Rug Making at the Kuhlmann-King House (12:30 PM - 2:00 PM CDT, Kuhlmann-King Historic House Museum).
  • Honey Creek Guided Walk - at Guadalupe River State Park (9:00 AM - 11:30 AM CDT).
  • Kuhlmann-King Historical Complex Free Tours (12:00 PM - 3:00 PM CDT, Kuhlmann-King Historical Complex).
  • Singer/Songwriter Live Music on the Patio @ Tapatio Springs (12:00 AM - 11:59 PM CDT).
  • St Mark Fall Fest. Families are invited from 9 a.m. to noon to enjoy a morning of seasonal fun at the Annual Fall Fest at St. Mark Presbyterian Church, 208 W. Bandera Road. Guests can enjoy food, games, prizes, raffles and festive activities for all ages.
  • Trunk or Treat at Texas Roadhouse Leon Springs (11:00 AM - 2:00 PM CDT). Join Texas Roadhouse-Leon Springs, 23102 W. I-10, from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. for their Fall Market, which will be combined with a Trunk- or-Treat. RSVP to: https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/phn5CYLcUK?origin=lprLink

Sunday, October 25

  • Halloween Fall Festival and Trunk or Treat at St. John Lutheran Church (12:00 PM - 1:45 PM CDT). If you are looking for some Halloween fun for the whole family join St. John Lutheran Church, 316 Rosewood Ave., from noon to 2 p.m. for an afternoon filled with food, candy, games and activities for all ages.
  • Honey Creek Guided Walk - at Guadalupe River State Park (9:00 AM - 11:30 AM CDT).

Friday night is also football night: check The Kendall Wire's Friday Night Finals roundup for Boerne High and Champion High scores.

Planning ahead? Watch for the next edition of the Boerne Star's Calendar of Events (https://www.boernestar.com/article/14374,calendar-of-events), and the Greater Boerne Chamber of Commerce calendar (https://business.boerne.org/events).
