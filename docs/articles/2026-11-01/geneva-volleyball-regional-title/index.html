Geneva Volleyball Wins Regional Title, Advances to State

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 1, 2026

Geneva School of Boerne won the regional volleyball title Saturday, beating Round Rock Christian in four sets in San Marcos, the Boerne Star reported October 31.

The Eagles won 25-22, 25-14, 22-25, 25-16 and advance to the Texas Association of Private and Parochial Schools (TAPPS) state tournament this coming week.

In Boerne ISD playoff action, Boerne Champion plays Medina Valley on Tuesday, November 3, at Northside Sports Gym at 6:30 p.m., and Boerne High plays Fox Tech on Tuesday at Blossom at 7:30 p.m., according to the Star.

What happens next: Geneva plays at the TAPPS state tournament this week, and the Boerne ISD teams play their playoff matches Tuesday.
