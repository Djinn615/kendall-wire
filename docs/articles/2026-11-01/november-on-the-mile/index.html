November on the Mile: Diva Night and Handmade Market Lead the Month

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 1, 2026

November on the Hill Country Mile starts with Fall Diva Night on Thursday, November 5, from 5 to 9 p.m., the Greater Boerne Chamber of Commerce announced. Downtown shops stay open late for the evening of shopping, dining, and drinks, with check-in at Main Plaza and Gunpowder & Cabernet.

The Boerne Handmade Market follows November 6-7 at 1910 Church, 130 Sisterdale Road, with more than 75 handmade-only vendors. A VIP night runs Friday, and the market is open to the public Saturday from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.

Pink Friday, a shop-small event, lands November 20, with 30 local businesses taking part and a Pink Friday Duck Hunt scavenger hunt, according to CityBuzz. The month closes with Dickens on Main on November 27-28, with Plaid Friday on November 27 and Small Business Saturday on November 28.
