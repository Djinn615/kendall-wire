Operation Christmas Child collection week runs through November 23 in Boerne

Operation Christmas Child collection week runs November 16 to 23, and St. John Lutheran Church, 315 Rosewood Avenue in Boerne, is the Kendall County area collection site for gift-filled shoeboxes, per the Boerne Star report published November 18.

Operation Christmas Child is a project of Samaritan's Purse that delivers gift-filled shoeboxes to children around the world, per Samaritan's Purse. The boxes are collected each year during National Collection Week, which Samaritan's Purse lists as November 16 to 23, 2026.

St. John Lutheran Church listed the same collection week dates, November 16 to 23, in its October 6 newsletter to members. The newsletter stated that shoeboxes could be picked up at the church starting October 18, that a box can also be filled online, and that volunteers are needed during collection week, with Gloria Baker named as the contact.

Collection hours for the Boerne site are listed on the Samaritan's Purse drop-off locations page, per the Star.

What happens next: the collection ends Monday, November 23, 2026, the last day of National Collection Week, per Samaritan's Purse and the church newsletter.
