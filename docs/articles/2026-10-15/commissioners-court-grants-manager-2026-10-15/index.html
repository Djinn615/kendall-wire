A new grants manager is hired, but the court never said the name

At a special called meeting Thursday, October 15, the court voted 4-0 to offer the new Grants Manager and Compliance Director position to its top-ranked finalist: classification 17 step 2B, salary $88,552.55. Yes votes: Stolarczyk, McCall, Wisian, Chapman. Carpenter was absent. The motion did not name the hire publicly; the pick was made in closed session (9:13-10:35 a.m., edited out of the published video). The role replaces the old grant coordinator position, the one a former employee complained about at the Oct 13 meeting. Thirty-five people applied; two finalists interviewed after one withdrew. The court also read out the full benefits package: county health coverage at no cost to the employee and TCDRS retirement with a 200% employer match starting in January. No other votes taken.
