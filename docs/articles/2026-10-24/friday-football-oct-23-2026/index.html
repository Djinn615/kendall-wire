Greyhounds stay perfect at 8-0 after pole fire delays homecoming win

Boerne High beat Veterans Memorial 49-7 on Friday for homecoming to stay 8-0 overall and 4-0 in district, per the Boerne Star. A fire on a light pole in the parking lot at halftime halted the game with Boerne leading 35-7. The stadium lights had to be turned off while the problem was fixed, and the game resumed at 10:25 p.m. on a running clock, ending at about 11 p.m.

Boerne scored on all five first-half possessions. Grant Sweeney threw five touchdown passes, four to Zach Freasier and one to Jacob Hicks. Kole Traylor returned the second-half kickoff for a touchdown to make it 42-7. Head coach Brett Sawyer told the Star, "It was a weird night, but district wins are hard to get and to get one is a blessing."

Boerne is on the road Friday at New Braunfels.

In the other Friday results, Boerne Champion led Smithson Valley 17-10 at halftime but lost 35-24, after the Rangers outscored the Chargers 25-7 in the second half behind three second-half turnovers. Champion is 7-1 overall and 5-1 in district, and hosts Austin McCallum on Friday for homecoming.

Comfort beat Karnes City 39-0 for its first win since August. The Bobcats are 2-6 overall and 1-3 in district, and host YMLA on Friday.

Geneva School of Boerne held off Holy Cross 23-21 at home. The Eagles are 8-1 overall and 3-1 in district, and close the regular season Friday at St. Francis Episcopal.
