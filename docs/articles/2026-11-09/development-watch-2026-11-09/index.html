TxDOT to update residents on Texas 46 West safety work

Texas Department of Transportation officials will give an update on the Texas 46 West safety project at a public session Tuesday, November 10, at 6:30 p.m. at the Patrick Heath Public Library, 451 N. Main St.

Precinct 1 Commissioner Jennifer McCall told the Kendall County Commissioners Court on October 13 that TxDOT officials would attend the session, according to the Boerne Star.

The county set aside $100,000 in its 2026-27 budget for improvements at Webster's Corner, the site of some of the highway's most severe crashes. The work planned there includes Jersey barriers, crash cushions, pavement widening and guard fence work. The money was the first local funding committed to the 46 West safety project and the first formal partnership between the county and TxDOT on the corridor.

TxDOT figures show 550 reported crashes on the 11-mile stretch between Interstate 10 and Texas 16 near Bandera from 2016 through March 2026. Ten of those were fatal wrecks that killed 13 people and left 44 others injured.

TxDOT has set September 2027 as the start date for work at Webster's Corner, a date commissioners hope to move up.

TxDOT's project pages for FM 3351 and for US 281 and SH 46 showed no changes this week.

Boerne City Council meets Tuesday, November 10, and the Zoning Board of Adjustment and Appeals meets Wednesday, November 18.

What happens next: TxDOT gives its update Tuesday evening at the library, and the Zoning Board of Adjustment and Appeals meets November 18.
