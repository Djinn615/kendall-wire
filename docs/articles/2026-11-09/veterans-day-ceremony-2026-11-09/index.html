Veterans Day ceremony set for Wednesday at Veterans Plaza

Boerne's Veterans of Foreign Wars Post 688 and American Legion Post 313 will hold a joint Veterans Day observance Wednesday, November 11, at 11 a.m. at Veterans Plaza, 801 S. Main St., according to the Boerne Star calendar of events.

The ceremony takes place at the 11th hour of the 11th day of the 11th month and runs until noon. It honors local men and women who served in the U.S. Armed Forces, per The Boerne Life. Veterans, their families, and friends are welcome.

Veterans Plaza is the site of the city's annual Veterans Day and Memorial Day ceremonies, per the City of Boerne.

What happens next: the observance runs Wednesday, November 11, from 11 a.m. to noon at Veterans Plaza.
