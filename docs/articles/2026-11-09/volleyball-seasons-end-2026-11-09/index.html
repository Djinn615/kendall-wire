Three Boerne-area volleyball seasons end Friday

The season ended Friday for all three Boerne-area volleyball teams, each in its own playoff bracket, the Boerne Star reported November 6.

The Geneva School of Boerne fell to Brazos Christian in the TAPPS 4A state semifinal in Waco, losing in four sets, 29-27, 25-19, 25-27, 25-23. Geneva won the third set. It was the program's 10th trip to the state tournament, per the Star.

Boerne Champion lost a five-set third-round match to Mission Sharyland in Alice, 25-15, 25-18, 18-25, 22-25, 15-12, per the Star. Sharyland took the first two sets, Champion answered by winning the third and fourth, and Sharyland closed the fifth 15-12. Champion finished 33-8, per MaxPreps. It was the Chargers' best playoff run since 2019, per the Star.

Boerne High was swept by Sharyland Pioneer in straight sets Friday in Laredo, 25-20, 25-21, 25-22, per RGVSports.com. Boerne High finished 31-7, per MaxPreps. It was the Greyhounds' best postseason run since 2015, per the Star.

What happens next: all three teams' seasons are over.
