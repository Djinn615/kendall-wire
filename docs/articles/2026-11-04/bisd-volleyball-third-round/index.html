Boerne Champion and Boerne High sweep into third round

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 4, 2026

Boerne Champion and Boerne High both swept their second-round volleyball matches Tuesday, moving both Boerne ISD teams into the third round, the Boerne Star reported November 3.

Champion swept Medina Valley at the Northside Sports Gym, winning 25-19, 25-21, and 25-19, per the Star. The win improved Champion to 33-7, per the Star. The October 31 Star playoff listing had Champion scheduled against Medina Valley at the Northside Sports Gym.

Boerne High swept Fox Tech at Blossom, winning 25-14, 25-14, and 25-19, per the Star. The win improved Boerne High to 31-6, per the Star. The October 31 Star playoff listing had Boerne High scheduled against Fox Tech at Blossom.

What happens next: Champion plays the winner of the Sharyland and Harlingen South match this weekend, per the Star. Boerne High plays the winner of the Pioneer and Pace match this weekend, per the Star.
