Geneva opens playoffs Saturday at Dallas Christian

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 4, 2026

Geneva School of Boerne opens the playoffs Saturday at 2 p.m. against Dallas Christian, the Boerne Star reported November 4. The game is listed at Wheeler Field in Mesquite, per the txhsfb.net playoff schedule.

Geneva enters at 9-1 after finishing second in its district, per the Star. Dallas Christian enters at 8-2 as district champion, per the Star. The bracket listing from the Lone Star College Sports Network also lists Geneva as the district second-place team traveling to district champion Dallas Christian.

Geneva won state in 2019, per the Star. Coach Adam Foren told the Star the 2019 title team finished the regular season at 6-4 before ending at 10-4, a worse regular-season record than this year's 9-1 team.

"They're well coached and have a good team, but our focus is on 2026," Foren told the Star. "We've played in a tough district and that has prepared us well. We're going to have to play well but we're battle tested, that's the reason we scheduled like we did."

Foren told the Star the Chargers are led by junior wide receiver Dylan Belt, who wears No. 4 and is being recruited by the University of Houston, Rice, UCF, and UT-El Paso.

"Their answer is getting the ball to No. 4, they run it well, but it starts and ends with getting the ball to No. 4," Foren told the Star. "Stopping their No. 1 weapon is our top priority."

Geneva is led by all-state players Caleb Quitadamo and Shae McNew, per the Star. All-state player Reid Aderholt was recently hurt, and his status for Saturday is uncertain, per the Star. Foren told the Star, "We scored 45 points a game for a reason."

"This has the feel of a state semifinal or a state championship game, but we're used to being in these games," Foren told the Star. "We won't be intimidated and they won't be intimidated. These are the ones you get excited for. They're a good team and we're a good team."

What happens next: Geneva plays Dallas Christian on Saturday, November 7, at 2 p.m. at Wheeler Field in Mesquite.
