Boerne ISD trustees named 2026 Outstanding School Board

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 17, 2026

The Boerne ISD Board of Trustees has been named the 2026 Outstanding School Board by the Texas Association of School Administrators, the top honor in the association's School Board Awards program.

It is the first time Boerne ISD has won the award. The board was a finalist once before, in 2003.

The trustees were chosen from five finalist boards interviewed by a committee of Texas school administrators. The other finalists were Alice, Denver City, Lovejoy, and Tomball ISDs.

The committee praised what it called the board's "one team mentality," its practice of rotating officer positions, and the district's Citizen Leadership Academy, which develops future board members from the community.

Superintendent Kristin Craft nominated the board for the award. The trustees serving at the time of the nomination: President Courtney Darter-Bruce, Vice President Rich Sena, Secretary Sarah Faulkenberry, and trustees Carlin Friar, Dallas Pipes, Garrett Wilson, and Kristi Schmidt.

Boerne ISD serves more than 11,200 students across 14 campuses, covering most of Kendall County and parts of Bexar and Comal counties.
