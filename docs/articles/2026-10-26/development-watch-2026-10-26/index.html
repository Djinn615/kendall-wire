Commissioners to weigh Corley Road closeout, Comfort setback request Tuesday

The Kendall County Commissioners Court meets Tuesday, October 27, at 9:00 a.m. in the Commissioners Courtroom on the 3rd floor of the Kendall County Courthouse, 201 East San Antonio Avenue, Boerne, with three Corley Road Phase 1 items and a Comfort setback relief request on its posted agenda.

The court will consider accepting the work comprising the Corley Road Phase 1 roadway and drainage improvements, built on the 1.157-acre tract described in the recorded right-of-way dedication, Document No. 2025-400322, which connects Corley Road with Scenic Loop Road, for maintenance. A second item would accept a warranty bond, a guarantee covering upkeep of street and drainage improvements, in the amount of $350,711.43 for one year. A third item would release a performance bond, a guarantee held to secure required road and drainage improvements, in the amount of $2,338,076.20. The agenda names Pulte Homes of Texas LP as developer, with Trey Rogers listed in that role, for the bond items.

The court will also consider a request for relief from Section 5.2.A.1 of the Kendall County Development Rules, which requires setbacks of 25 feet on all sides of a property. The request seeks setbacks of 0 feet on three sides and 5 feet on one side for a proposed development at 714 High Street in Comfort, approximately 215 feet east of Seventh Street. The agenda names 714 High Street, LLC, as owner, with Belinda Kay Pence listed in that role.

Earlier in October, the posted agenda for October 13 listed a request to release the financial guarantee for the Miralomas Garden Homes Subdivision Unit 3, in the form of a cash deposit of $1,166,589.55 for required street and drainage improvements. The agenda named Miralomas Development Corporation as owner. That agenda also listed an amending plat (a revised official map of a subdivision's lots) creating Lot 1A for Tiny Homes Patriot Academy.

No City of Boerne Planning and Zoning cases were listed among the upcoming events on the city Legistar calendar.

On the TxDOT side, the FM 3351 corridor project, running from I-10 to SH 46, remains in environmental studies and schematic design, with a 10-foot shared-use path planned on the west side of FM 3351. TxDOT is proposing to widen FM 3351 from Desperado Way to SH 46. At US 281 and SH 46, nighttime paving for the final surface of SH 46 continues, with traffic signal installation and roadside work remaining.

For SH 46 West, the October 13 county agenda included an update on Resolution No. R-20260428-2, in which the court asked TxDOT to address roadway safety on SH 46 West. The Boerne Star reported that TxDOT officials will give an update on the SH 46 West safety project at a 6:30 p.m. session November 10 at the Patrick Heath Public Library, 451 N. Main St.

What happens next: the Commissioners Court takes up the Corley Road and Comfort items at its October 27 meeting, and the TxDOT session on SH 46 West is set for November 10.
