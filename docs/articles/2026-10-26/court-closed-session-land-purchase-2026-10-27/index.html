Court to discuss 120 acres near Comfort in closed session Tuesday

Kendall County Commissioners Court will meet in closed session Tuesday, October 27, to discuss a possible purchase of 120 acres near Comfort, Texas, previously owned by the Hawkins family. The meeting starts at 9 a.m. in the Commissioners Courtroom on the third floor of the Kendall County Courthouse, 201 East San Antonio Avenue, Boerne.

The item, listed as item 37 on the posted agenda, calls for a closed session on matters related to the Proposition A Committee. Those matters concern the purchase, exchange, lease, or value of real property. The agenda cites Section 551.072 of the Texas Open Meetings Act, which covers deliberation regarding real property in a closed meeting. The agenda states the closed session is authorized when public discussion would harm the county's negotiating position with a third party.

Item 37.a describes the subject as discussion regarding the purchase of 120 acres near Comfort, previously owned by the Hawkins family.

What the agenda does not list matters here. No seller is named in the agenda items, and no price is listed. The item is listed for discussion in closed session, not as a purchase vote. A post in a local Facebook group over the weekend described the item with claims about a seller and a price. Those claims do not appear in the posted agenda.

What happens next: after the closed session, the agenda lists item 42, consideration and possible action regarding matters discussed in closed session, once the court reconvenes in open session.
