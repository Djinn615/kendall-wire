Greyhounds clinch district title, Chargers lock up second place

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 2, 2026

The Boerne Greyhounds clinched the District 13-5A Division II title Friday, beating New Braunfels 28-17 on the road, the Boerne Star reported October 30. It is the Greyhounds sixth district title in seven years.

Boerne opened the scoring on a trick play late in the first quarter. Receiver Jacob Hicks took the ball on a second reverse and hit quarterback Grant Sweeney for a 63-yard score and a 7-0 lead.

New Braunfels tied the game, then took a 17-14 lead early in the second half on a 3-yard run by backup quarterback Kane Bunch. Boerne answered 36 seconds later when Sweeney connected with receiver Lane Aaron on a 44-yard touchdown pass for a 21-17 lead.

Up 21-17 early in the fourth quarter, Boerne faced fourth-and-13 at the New Braunfels 37-yard line. Sweeney, lined up to punt, instead threw to Hicks for 17 yards and a first down. Three plays later, tight end Beau Dayoc caught a 1-yard pass for the 28-17 final.

Sweeney finished 9 of 17 passing for 135 yards, and his three touchdown passes gave him 32 on the season against three interceptions, per the Star. Running back Callen Combs topped 1,000 rushing yards for the season.

The undefeated Greyhounds are 9-0 overall and 5-0 in district. They close the regular season November 6, hosting the Legacy Ranch Wranglers at Boerne ISD Stadium.

The same night, Boerne Champion beat the Austin McCallum Knights 61-0 on homecoming at Boerne ISD Stadium, the Star reported. The win secured second place in District 13-5A Division I for the Chargers, who improved to 8-1 overall and 6-1 in district.

Second place earns Champion a home game in the first round of the playoffs. The Chargers close the regular season Friday at Manor, then host a playoff game the following week at Boerne ISD Stadium, with the date and time to be announced.
