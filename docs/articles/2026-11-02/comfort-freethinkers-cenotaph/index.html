Comfort's freethinkers cenotaph was dedicated 24 years ago

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 2, 2026

On November 2, 2002, a limestone cenotaph honoring the founding freethinkers of Comfort was dedicated in front of the Ingenhuett Store on High Street, 24 years ago today. More than 100 people attended on a cold, rainy day.

The dedication ended a five-year controversy. An earlier, much larger freethought monument placed in Comfort Park in 1998 was removed in late 1999 after an outcry over its atheist funding. The replacement eight-foot monument was built by Karl H. Kuhn of Boerne and paid for by freethinker sponsors.

Comfort's German founders were freethinkers who supported abolition, women's suffrage, and secular schools, convictions that set the town apart on the Texas frontier and shaped its Unionist stand during the Civil War.
