Kendall County election results (unofficial)

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 3, 2026

Update 7:37 PM

Polls closed at 7 PM on November 3. The first unofficial Kendall County figures posted to the Texas Secretary of State Election Night Returns portal cover early votes, with 1 of 18 precincts reporting (5.56 percent). Results are unofficial and will change as more precincts report.

In U.S. House District 21, Mark Teixeira has 14,607 votes (73.42 percent) and Kristin Hook has 5,289 votes (26.58 percent), out of 19,896 votes counted in the district totals shown for Kendall County, per the portal.

In State House District 19, Ellen Troxclair has 14,537 votes (73.97 percent) and Tom Luck has 5,116 votes (26.03 percent), out of 19,653 votes counted in the district totals shown for Kendall County, per the portal.

Statewide, in the U.S. Senate race, James Talarico has 2,200,336 votes and Ken Paxton has 1,449,895 votes, with 35.55 percent of precincts reporting (3,519 of 9,900), per the Associated Press. The Associated Press rates the race too early to call.

No winner is called in any of these races. With 17 of 18 Kendall County precincts still to report, the remaining ballots could change the county totals.

Local results for Justice of the Peace Precinct 1, Comfort ISD Trustee District 2, and the Comal ISD bond propositions have not posted on the county election page, and those contests are not listed in the portal's Kendall County race list. This article will add those figures when the county posts them.

County turnout documents show 19,099 people voted in person during early voting, and the county mail ballot roster shows 1,054 mail ballots accepted. The county lists 41,263 registered voters.

More results will be added as the county and the state portal post them through the night.
