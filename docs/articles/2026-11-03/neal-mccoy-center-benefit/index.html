Neal McCoy to headline Center benefit concert Saturday

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 3, 2026

Neal McCoy will headline "Starry Night Beneath the Hill Country Sky," the annual benefit concert for The Center, on Saturday, November 7, at Estancia at Thunder Valley, according to The Center's benefit concert page.

Doors open at 6 p.m., and McCoy takes the stage at 8:50 p.m., the Boerne Star reported November 3. Dinner from Don Strange Catering and a live auction are part of the evening. San Antonio jazz vocalist Faith Evangeline and the Dirty River Jazz Band open the evening, per the Star.

The concert is in its second year. Last year it drew 400 guests and raised more than 170,000 dollars, per the Star. "Last year we traded the gala for a night under the stars, and this community showed up," Olivia Burdick, CEO of The Center, told the Star.

This is not a public concert in the usual sense. Guests attend as sponsors and the tables they invite, per the Star, and a limited number of individual tickets are available.

Proceeds support The Center's work, including Meals on Wheels, daily lunches, wellness and social programming, the medical equipment loan closet, and resource navigation for families, per the Star. The Center is the only Meals on Wheels provider in Kendall County, and last year it delivered more than 58,000 meals, each paired with a wellness check and a few minutes of conversation at the door, per the Star.

McCoy is a Texas native with five No. 1 hits across three charts and a career spanning more than three decades, per the Star. His chart-toppers include "No Doubt About It" and "Wink." He is a member of the Texas Country Music Hall of Fame, a 2026 inductee into the Texas Cowboy Hall of Fame, and the newest member of the Grand Ole Opry, per the Star.

What happens next: the benefit concert takes place Saturday, November 7, at Estancia at Thunder Valley. Details are listed on The Center's benefit concert page.
