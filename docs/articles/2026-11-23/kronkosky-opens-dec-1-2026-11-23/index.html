Kronkosky State Natural Area opens December 1

The Albert and Bessie Kronkosky State Natural Area outside Boerne opens to the public for day use on December 1, and overnight stays start December 15, Texas Parks and Wildlife said in its November 18 news release.

The natural area covers about 3,800 acres, with 26 miles of trails and 31 campsites, per the release. Overnight options run from mini cabins to primitive backcountry tent sites.

Day use passes are $6 for visitors ages 13 and older, per the release, and the site is open from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m., seven days a week. The reservation system opens December 1 for both day use and camping, and the department recommends reservations because day use capacity may be reached.

Superintendent Steph Kwiatkowski said in the release that the site offers trail experiences with a range of difficulty, width, and surface type. The release states the site is the first in the Texas state parks system to feature an all-terrain wheelchair, and its Nature Center offers braille, narrated audio guides, and tactile exhibits.

Albert and Bessie Kronkosky began buying property in the area in 1946, per the release, and willed the land to the state of Texas to protect it. Texas Parks and Wildlife took ownership of the ranch in March 2011.

What happens next: Day use begins December 1, reservations open the same day, and overnight stays begin December 15.
