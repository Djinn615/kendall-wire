Freeze Warning for Kendall County until 9:00 AM on November 25

The National Weather Service office in Austin/San Antonio has issued a Freeze Warning that includes Kendall County.

FREEZE WARNING NOW IN EFFECT FROM MIDNIGHT TUESDAY NIGHT TO 9 AM CST WEDNESDAY

  • WHAT...Sub-freezing temperatures between 26 and 32 expected.
  • WHERE...Bandera, Blanco, Burnet, Edwards, Gillespie, Kendall, Kerr, Llano, and Real Counties.
  • WHEN...From midnight Tuesday Night to 9 AM CST Wednesday.
  • IMPACTS...Frost and freeze conditions could kill crops, other sensitive vegetation and possibly damage unprotected outdoor plumbing.

Instruction from the National Weather Service: Take steps now to protect tender plants from the cold. To prevent water pipes from freezing; wrap or drain or allow them to drip slowly.
