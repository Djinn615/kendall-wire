Boerne completes perfect regular season with 32-27 win over Legacy Ranch

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 7, 2026

Boerne High completed a perfect regular season Friday night, beating Liberty Hill Legacy Ranch 32-27 in a game that came down to the final play.

Boerne trailed 14-12 at halftime, then scored all 20 of its second-half points in the third quarter. The Greyhounds had the ball only once in the fourth quarter.

After a Boerne punt late in the fourth quarter, Legacy Ranch took over at its own 18-yard line with 4:36 left, trailing 25-20, and drove deep into Boerne territory. The Wranglers converted a fourth down at the 9-yard line with five seconds left, out of timeouts, then threw a pass into the end zone that Kole Traylor intercepted with no time on the clock. It was the 19th play of the drive.

Traylor finished with two interceptions in the game. He returned one to the Wranglers 20-yard line, and the Boerne offense converted it for a touchdown.

Boerne has finished 10-0 in the regular season only once before, in 2022, when the Greyhounds advanced to the state championship game.

Boerne Champion closed out its regular season at Manor, beating the Mustangs 49-7. The Chargers finish 9-1 and second in their district.

Comfort lost its regular-season finale to Blanco 74-29, ending the Bobcats season. Comfort needed to beat Blanco by 18 points to have a chance at the postseason.

What happens next: Boerne hosts Brackenridge at BISD Stadium on Thursday to open the playoffs. Champion plays Friday at Boerne ISD Stadium against either MacArthur or Laredo Nixon to open the 5A-DI playoffs. Geneva begins its postseason Saturday afternoon at Dallas Christian in the first round of the TAPPS playoffs.
