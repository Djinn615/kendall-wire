Boerne PD kicks off Blue Santa toy drive

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 7, 2026

The Boerne Police Department has kicked off its annual Blue Santa Program and is now accepting toy donations and applications from families in need.

Blue Santa is a collaboration between the Boerne Police Department, Boerne Fire Department, Fair Oaks Ranch Police Department, Kendall County Sheriff's Office, Hill Country Family Services, American Legion Post 313, VFW Post 688, and Boerne ISD. The program provides Christmas gifts to children who might otherwise go without.

Families in need can stop by the Kendall County Sheriff's Office or Hill Country Family Services to complete an application. The deadline to apply is December 15. To help as many families as possible, the program cannot fulfill requests made through multiple organizations.

Anyone wanting to help can donate new, unwrapped toys for boys and girls from infants through 17 years old, or gift cards from local businesses in Boerne and Comfort.

Donations can be dropped off through December 14 at the Kendall County Sheriff's Office, 6 Staudt St.; Boerne Police Department, 124 Old San Antonio Rd.; Boerne Fire Department, 726 N Main St.; Fair Oaks Ranch Police Department, 7286 Dietz Elkhorn Rd.; Black Rifle Coffee, 101 South Main St.; Apache Rifle Works, both locations; and Hill Country Family Services, 118 W Advogt St.
