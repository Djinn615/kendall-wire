Champion beats Eagle Pass Winn 72-0 in playoff opener

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 14, 2026

Boerne Champion beat Eagle Pass C. C. Winn 72-0 on Friday night, November 13, at Boerne ISD Stadium in the opening round of the Class 5A Division I playoffs.

The win improved Champion to 10-1. Winn finished its season at 4-7. The score is also listed by High School Football America in its roundup of opening round finals from November 12-14.

Champion entered the postseason at 9-1. Winn reached the playoffs after finishing district play at 4-3, with three straight wins late in the regular season over San Antonio Jay, South San Antonio and Laredo Nixon, the Eagle Pass Business Journal reported.

What happens next for Champion: an area round game against McAllen High. McAllen beat Weslaco East 62-19 on Friday night in McAllen after trailing 19-0 in the first quarter, and improved to 11-0. The Champion and McAllen game is set for 7 p.m. Thursday, November 19, at the Student Activity Center in Laredo, according to the RGVSports.com area round schedule.

Boerne High also learned its area round opponent on Friday night. Mercedes beat Mission Veterans Memorial 44-42 at Tom Landry Stadium in Mission, according to the RGVSports.com bi-district score list. Mercedes advances to play Boerne in the Class 5A Division II area round. Boerne beat San Antonio Brackenridge 56-14 on Thursday night and is 11-0.
