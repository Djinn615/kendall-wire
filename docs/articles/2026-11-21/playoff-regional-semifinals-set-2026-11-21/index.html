Regional semifinal matchups set for Boerne and Champion

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 21, 2026

Both Boerne ISD football teams learned their regional semifinal opponents on Friday night, November 20, and both games are rematches from the regular season.

Champion faces Austin Anderson

Austin Anderson beat Corpus Christi Veterans Memorial 58-29 in the area round on Friday night, according to Dave Campbell's Texas Football. Anderson improved to 10-2.

Boerne Champion, 11-1, beat McAllen High 72-14 on Thursday night in Laredo. Champion beat Anderson 61-32 on September 25, according to the Champion schedule on MaxPreps.

The Class 5A Division I regional semifinal between Anderson and Champion is set for 2:00 p.m. on Friday, November 27, at Shelton Stadium in Buda, according to Dave Campbell's Texas Football.

Boerne faces Legacy Ranch

Liberty Hill Legacy Ranch beat Corpus Christi Ray 31-0 in the area round on Friday night, according to Dave Campbell's Texas Football. Legacy Ranch improved to 7-5.

Boerne, 12-0, beat Mercedes 41-7 on Thursday night in Laredo. Boerne beat Legacy Ranch 32-27 on November 6, according to the Boerne schedule on MaxPreps.

The Class 5A Division II regional semifinal between Boerne and Legacy Ranch is set for 1:00 p.m. on Friday, November 27, at Gupton Stadium in Cedar Park, according to Dave Campbell's Texas Football.

Both games are scheduled for Friday, November 27, the day after Thanksgiving.
