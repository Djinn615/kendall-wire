Greyhounds and Chargers Both Win Big: Friday Night Finals, October 16

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 16, 2026

Both Boerne ISD varsity football teams were in action on Friday, October 16, and both came away with wins.

Boerne Greyhounds 49, Liberty Hill 7

The Boerne Greyhounds beat Liberty Hill 49-7 on the road in conference play on Friday, October 16. The win moves the Greyhounds to 7-0 on the season.

Champion Chargers 57, Seguin 7

The Champion Chargers beat Seguin 57-7 at home in conference play on Friday, October 16. The win moves the Chargers to 7-0 on the season.

Next up

Boerne Greyhounds hosts Veterans Memorial on Friday, October 23; Champion Chargers travels to Smithson Valley on Friday, October 23. Kickoff is set for 7 p.m.
