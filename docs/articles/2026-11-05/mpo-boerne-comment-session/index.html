MPO brings road plan comment session to Boerne November 18

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 5, 2026

Boerne is one of five stops as the Alamo Area Metropolitan Planning Organization asks the public to weigh in on approximately 39.2 billion dollars in proposed transportation investments in its Mobility 2050 Update. The organization brings its public comment session to the Patrick Heath Public Library, 451 N. Main St., at 5 p.m. November 18.

The plan identifies proposed investments in roadways, transit, walking and cycling connections, and other transportation needs. Also available for public review is the 2027 Transportation Conformity Determination, which evaluates whether the transportation investments in the plan meet federal air quality requirements.

"The Mobility 2050 Update outlines approximately 39.2 billion dollars in proposed transportation investments across our region," said Brendon Wheeler, the organization's executive director. "Public input helps us understand whether the proposed investments reflect the transportation needs and priorities of the people who live and work here," Wheeler said.

What happens next: those unable to attend can participate online at alamoareampo.org/YourMove. The plan documents are available for review online November 1 through December 26. The final draft will be considered for adoption at the January 25, 2027 Transportation Policy Board meeting.
