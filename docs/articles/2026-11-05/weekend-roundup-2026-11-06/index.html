This Weekend in Boerne and Kendall County: November 6-8

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 5, 2026

Here is what is on around Boerne and Kendall County from Friday, November 6 to Sunday, November 8.

Friday, November 6

  • Community Rag Rug Making at Patrick Heath Public Library (10:00 AM - 12:00 PM CST, Patrick Heath Public Library, 451 N. Main St.). Children and families work together on a braided rag rug to be displayed at the Kuhlmann-King Historic House Museum.
  • Fall Boerne Handmade Market at 1910 Church (10:00 AM - 9:00 PM CST, 1910 Church, 130 Sisterdale Rd). More than 75 handmade-only vendors. VIP night 6:30-9:30 p.m., $25.
  • First Friday Parents' Night Out (6:00 PM - 8:00 PM CST, St. Mark Presbyterian Church, 208 W. Bandera Rd.). Free childcare for newborns through 5th grade.
  • First Friday Yoga for Adults at Patrick Heath Public Library (6:00 PM - 7:00 PM CST, Patrick Heath Public Library, 451 N. Main St.).
  • Free-to-Play Fridays at Patrick Heath Public Library (4:00 PM - 6:00 PM CST, Patrick Heath Public Library, 451 N. Main St.).
  • Game Day Friday at Lost Oak Cellars (4:00 PM - 7:00 PM CST, Lost Oak Cellars, 111 Rosewood Ave).
  • Hill Country Rib Night (5:00 PM - 9:00 PM CST).
  • Live Music at The Dodging Duck Brewhaus (5:30 PM - 8:30 PM CST, The Dodging Duck Brewhaus, 402 River Rd).
  • Public Memory Lab Open at Patrick Heath Public Library (10:00 AM - 2:00 PM CST, Patrick Heath Public Library, 451 N. Main St.).
  • Singer/Songwriter Live Music on the Patio @ Tapatio Springs (12:00 AM - 11:59 PM CST).
  • Tai Chi with Qi gong (10:00 AM - 11:00 AM CST, Patrick Heath Public Library, 451 N. Main St.).

Saturday, November 7

  • 2nd Annual Private & Homeschool Fair (1:00 PM - 3:00 PM CST, St. John Lutheran Church).
  • A Taste of the Mariachi Extravaganza (7:00 PM - 10:00 PM CST, Cave Without A Name, 325 Kreutzberg Rd). Young vocalists from the Mariachi Extravaganza National Vocal Competition perform traditional mariachi music.
  • Boerne Band Boosters. Join the Boerne H.S. Band Boosters for their second annual Casino Night Fundraiser from 6-10 p.m. at Signarama, 23775 I-10 West, for an evening of casino games, great food, drinks, prizes, and fun. Details: https://www.greyhoundband.com/casino
  • Boerne Ghost Tours. Explore the darker side of Boerne's history during a 90-minute guided walking tour hosted by DeVino Cellars. The evening begins at 7:30 p.m. at DeVino, 112 E. San Antonio Ave. Tickets start at $28.52, at: https://www.eventbrite.com/e/boerne-ghost-tours-bydevino-cellars-presented-by-theboerne-life-tickets-2000183292070
  • Boots & Bonfires at Tapatio Springs (6:00 PM - 9:00 PM CST, Tapatio Springs Hill Country Resort, Clubhouse Event Lawn). General admission $50, includes BBQ buffet.
  • Cibolo Farmers Market at Herff Farm (9:00 AM - 1:00 PM CST, HERFF FARM).
  • Community Fall Festival hosted by Town and Country Nursing & Rehabilitation Center (10:00 AM - 2:00 PM CST, Town and Country Nursing and Rehabilitation Center).
  • European Motor Showcase (10:00 AM - 3:00 PM CST). Open to the public, this family-friendly event will feature an impressive collection of luxury, exotic, classic, and modern European vehicles. Hosted from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. at Tapatio Springs Hill Country Resort, 1 Resort Way.
  • Fall Boerne Handmade Market at 1910 Church (9:00 AM - 4:00 PM CST, 1910 Church, 130 Sisterdale Rd). More than 75 handmade-only vendors. Admission $5.
  • First Saturdays in Boerne (5:00 PM - 8:00 PM CST).
  • Hill Country Rib Night (5:00 PM - 9:00 PM CST).
  • Holiday Market Day at the Ranch (10:00 AM - 2:30 PM CST, Cibolo Creek Community Church, 30395 Ralph Fair Rd., Fair Oaks Ranch). Fifty local vendors, food trucks, free admission.
  • Honey Creek Guided Walk - at Guadalupe River State Park (9:00 AM - 11:30 AM CST).
  • Honor the Brave Presented by TBI Warrior Foundation (5:00 PM - 10:00 PM CST).
  • Lone Star Mopar Fest. The weekend features scenic drives, social gatherings and a special MOPAR Main Event from 9:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. at Boerne's Main Plaza. Visitors can enjoy the vehicles on display before exploring the nearby shops, restaurants, and attractions along the Hill Country Mile.
  • ReCrafted Workshop and The Dream Project at Patrick Heath Public Library (10:30 AM CST, Patrick Heath Public Library Youth Department outdoor porch). A creative, eco-friendly workshop with ReCrafted, followed by The Dream Project, where Champion High School students guide children in making dream bracelets.
  • Singer/Songwriter Live Music on the Patio @ Tapatio Springs (12:00 AM - 11:59 PM CST).
  • The Grape Gathering at Ironwood Events (11:00 AM - 4:00 PM CST). Join Ironwood Events from 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. at Von Erich Ranch, 1637 FM 473. Wine, food, music and shopping all in one relaxed Hill Country afternoon. Tickets $35 per person, at: https://www.ticketsource.com/booking/select/opgvazeaamaz

Sunday, November 8

  • Honey Creek Guided Walk - at Guadalupe River State Park (9:00 AM - 11:30 AM CST).
