One killed, one hurt in head-on crash on SH-46 near Boerne

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 5, 2026

A head-on crash on State Highway 46 near Boerne killed one driver and injured another Wednesday around noon.

The Texas Department of Public Safety said the eastbound driver, a man from Pipe Creek, crossed into oncoming traffic and collided with a Mitsubishi. He died after being taken to a medical facility. The other driver was taken to a hospital, and DPS has not reported that driver's condition.

DPS Sgt. Dila Hidalgo told the San Antonio Express-News the eastbound car, a Mazda Miata, "crossed into the oncoming lane of traffic and collided with the Mitsubishi Outlander." The cause of the crash remains under investigation, and DPS has not said why the car crossed over.

DPS asks anyone who saw the crash to call 210-531-2284.

What happens next: DPS continues to investigate the crash. No finding has been released on why the car entered the other lane.
