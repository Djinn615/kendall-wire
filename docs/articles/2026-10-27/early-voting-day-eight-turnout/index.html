Early Voting Day Eight: Monday Draws 1,500 Voters

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 27, 2026

Day eight of early voting is in the books for Kendall County. Monday, October 26 drew 1,500 in-person voters, up 966 from Sunday's 534.

The eight-day in-person total is 13,086, which the county puts at 31.68 percent turnout. The county's in-person table uses 41,302 registered voters; the county elections page lists 41,263.

Mail-ballot totals moved as well. Accepted mail ballots now total 811, up 30 from Sunday's 781. The county reports 1,363 mail ballots sent, 834 received, 14 pending, and 6 undeliverable.

Combined in-person plus accepted mail ballots: 13,897, about 33.7 percent of the 41,263 registered voters listed on the county elections page.

Early voting at the Courthouse Annex in Boerne runs 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. through Friday, October 30.

What happens next: early voting continues through Friday, October 30, and Election Day is Tuesday, November 3.
