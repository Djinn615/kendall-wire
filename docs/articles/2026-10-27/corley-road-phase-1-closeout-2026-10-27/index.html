Court closes out Corley Road Phase 1

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 27, 2026

The Kendall County Commissioners Court closed out Corley Road Phase 1 in four unanimous votes at its regular meeting Tuesday, October 27.

All four votes were 4-0, with County Judge Shane Stolarczyk and Commissioners Jennifer McCall, Andra Wisian, and Richard Chapman voting yes. Commissioner Chad Carpenter had left for the day before the afternoon session.

The court accepted the work comprising the Corley Road Phase 1 roadway and drainage improvements for maintenance. The work covers a 1.157-acre tract connecting Corley Road with Scenic Loop Road. The developer is Pulte Homes of Texas LP, with Trey Rogers. The motion was made by Commissioner McCall and seconded by Commissioner Wisian.

The court accepted a maintenance bond for the work in the form of a warranty bond of $350,711.43 for one year, also from Pulte Homes of Texas LP. The motion was made by Commissioner McCall and seconded by Commissioner Wisian.

The court released the financial guarantee for the work in the form of a performance bond of $2,338,076.20. The motion was made by Commissioner McCall.

The court relinquished any property interest the county may have in an unused 0.5914-acre parcel near Corley Road and Scenic Loop Road and authorized the county judge to execute a quitclaim deed or other legal instrument. The parcel was originally planned for Corley Road Phase 1 but was not used, and a different right of way was granted. The motion was made by Commissioner McCall and seconded by Commissioner Wisian.
