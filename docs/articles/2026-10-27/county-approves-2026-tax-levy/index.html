Commissioners approve 2026 property tax levy

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 27, 2026

The Kendall County Commissioners Court approved the 2026 property tax levy roll at its regular meeting Tuesday, October 27.

The levy totals $44,778,256.64. The vote was 5-0, with County Judge Shane Stolarczyk and Commissioners Jennifer McCall, Andra Wisian, Richard Chapman, and Chad Carpenter all voting yes. The motion was made by Stolarczyk and seconded by Chapman.

The total is up about $1,425,000 over last year, the Tax Assessor-Collector told the court.

The levy is the amount the county will collect from property taxes for the year.
