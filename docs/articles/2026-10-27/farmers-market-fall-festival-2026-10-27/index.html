Boerne Farmers Market turns into Fall Festival tonight

The regularly scheduled Boerne Farmers Market becomes a Fall Festival for the whole family tonight, Tuesday, October 27, from 4:00 to 7:00 PM at the Boerne Farmers Market, 157 S Main St.

Admission is free. The listing promises food vendors, crafts, a bounce house, music, and games for kids, plus free beer for ages 21 and up.

It is the regular farmer and artisan market, dressed up for fall, so expect the usual market vendors alongside the festival activities.
