Chargers Win District Title, Volleyball Playoffs Set

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 27, 2026

Boerne Champion went on the road and defeated Alamo Heights for the 26-5A volleyball title Friday night, the Boerne Star reported October 24.

The Chargers swept the Mules, 25-22, 25-20, 25-22, to wrap up their first district title since 2019 with an 11-1 district record. Champion is 31-7 overall.

The first-round playoff schedule is set for Thursday, October 29. Champion plays MacArthur at the Alamo Convocation Center at 8 p.m. Boerne High plays Seguin at Davenport High School at 6:30 p.m.

Geneva School of Boerne already opened the playoffs. The Eagles won their first-round match against Waco Live Oak Classical in straight sets, 26-24, 25-15, 25-20. Geneva plays Conroe Covenant in the next round, with details to be announced.

What happens next: the two Boerne ISD first-round matches are Thursday, October 29, and Geneva's next-round date is still to be announced.
