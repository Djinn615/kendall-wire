Court authorizes sheriff salary grant application, forensic subscription

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 27, 2026

The Kendall County Commissioners Court took two unanimous votes on Sheriff's Office funding at its regular meeting Tuesday, October 27.

The court authorized the county judge to apply to the Texas Comptroller for the Rural Sheriff's Office Salary Assistance Grant Program, known as SB 22, under Resolution R-20261027-1. The application requests funding of up to $350,000 for the Sheriff's Office for fiscal year 2027. The motion was made by County Judge Shane Stolarczyk and seconded by Commissioner Richard Chapman. The vote was 5-0, with Stolarczyk and Commissioners Jennifer McCall, Andra Wisian, Chapman, and Chad Carpenter all voting yes.

The court also approved a one-year agreement with up to two annual renewals with Carahsoft Technology Corporation for a Cellebrite Guardian Forensics and Pathfinder subscription for the Sheriff's Office. The subscription costs $214,071.66 and is funded through the federal OVW Cybercrimes Grant, using DIR Cooperative Contract DIR-CPO-5687. The motion was made by Commissioner Wisian and seconded by Commissioner Chapman. The vote was 4-0, with Stolarczyk, McCall, Wisian, and Chapman voting yes. Carpenter had left for the day before the afternoon session. The court was told the $214,071.66 figure is the total, not an annual amount that rises.
