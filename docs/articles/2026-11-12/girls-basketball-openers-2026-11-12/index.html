Boerne and Champion girls open basketball season with wins

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 12, 2026

Boerne High and Boerne Champion opened the girls basketball season with home wins, the Boerne Star reported on November 11.

Boerne High beat O'Connor 56-42 to start the year 1-0. The Lady Greyhounds led 15-9 after the first quarter, held a 19-18 lead at halftime, and outscored the Lady Panthers 19-6 in the third quarter.

Julie Benavidez led Boerne with 17 points. Addison Saur added 16 points, 8 rebounds and 11 steals, and Addison Neal scored 12, giving the Lady Greyhounds three players in double figures.

Champion beat Roosevelt 49-31 in its opener. The Lady Chargers trailed 10-7 after the first quarter and outscored the Rough Riders 42-21 over the final three quarters.

Brielle Burdick led Champion with 24 points. Jordyn Davis added 11 points, Maya Clark scored 8 and Brooke Burk scored 6.

The Geneva School of Boerne boys also opened with a win, beating Savio 57-37, a 20-point margin.

What happens next: Boerne High hosts Wall on Saturday at 2 p.m., and Champion plays in the Harlandale tournament this weekend.
