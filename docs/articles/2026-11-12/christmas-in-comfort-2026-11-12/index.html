Christmas in Comfort marks 40th year November 28

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 12, 2026

Christmas in Comfort returns to the Comfort Historic District on Saturday, November 28, for its 40th year, hosted by the Comfort Area Chamber of Commerce.

More than 100 arts and crafts vendors will set up along High Street and Seventh Street. High Street vendors are open from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., Seventh Street vendors from 10 a.m. to 9 p.m., and the food courts and drink booths at Seventh and High run from 10 a.m. to 9 p.m., according to the chamber schedule.

Live entertainment runs during the day on three stages, at Seventh and High, at Remedy Hall, and at the Comfort Backyard. Santa Claus is available from 11 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

First responders from the surrounding area will serve as special guests at the parade. Margaret Stone, a Comfort Chamber of Commerce board member and event organizer, told the Boerne Star the tribute answers two years of flood response in the Hill Country.

"We invite residents throughout the Hill Country to join us at the parade to express our gratitude for the service of our first responders," Stone said.

Stone also said the chamber has asked the Heritage Foundation for help finding the people who started the event 40 years ago, to bring them to this year's parade.

The evening closes with caroling and the lighting of the Comfort Community Christmas tree at 6:30 p.m. at Seventh and High streets, the lighted night parade at 7 p.m., and a fireworks display after the parade.

What happens next: Christmas in Comfort is Saturday, November 28. The full schedule is on the Comfort Area Chamber of Commerce website.
