Mail Ballots Accepted Rise to 848 in Kendall County

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 29, 2026

Kendall County reports 848 accepted mail ballots for the November 3 joint election, up 10 from the prior total of 838.

The county's updated mail-ballot total shows 1,363 ballots sent to voters and 872 received. Of those received, 848 were accepted, 14 are pending voter action to correct a defect on the carrier envelope, and 7 were undeliverable. The October 28 row in the county's mail-ballot breakdown shows 10 ballots received and 10 accepted.

No new in-person early voting day was posted with the mail-ballot update. The in-person total stands at 14,614 through Tuesday, October 27, which the county puts at 35.38 percent turnout. The county's in-person table uses 41,302 registered voters; the county elections page lists 41,263.

Combined, in-person votes plus accepted mail ballots total 15,462, about 37.5 percent of the 41,263 registered voters listed on the county elections page.

A same-day comparison to November 2025 early voting was not included because an equivalent-day figure could not be verified from a county or Secretary of State source.

Early voting continues at the Kendall County Courthouse Annex, 221 Fawn Valley Drive in Boerne, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. through Friday, October 30. The deadline to apply for a ballot by mail was Friday, October 23; the application had to be received by the county, not just postmarked. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, with polls open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.

What happens next: early voting continues through Friday, October 30, and Election Day is Tuesday, November 3.
