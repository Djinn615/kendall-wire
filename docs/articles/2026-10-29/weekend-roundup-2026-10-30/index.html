This Weekend in Boerne and Kendall County: October 30 to November 1

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 29, 2026

Here is what is on around Boerne and Kendall County from Friday, October 30 to Sunday, November 1.

Friday, October 30

  • Hill Country Rib Night (5:00 PM - 9:00 PM CDT).
  • Member Orientation - Presented by South Texas Radiology Imaging Center (11:30 AM - 1:00 PM CDT, The Greater Boerne Chamber of Commerce Boardroom).
  • Ribbon Cutting for Morningside Menger Springs Terrace Homes (9:00 AM - 9:30 AM CDT, Morningside Menger Springs).
  • Singer/Songwriter Live Music on the Patio @ Tapatio Springs (12:00 AM - 11:59 PM CDT).
  • Fall Festival at Boerne Church of Christ (6:00 PM - 8:00 PM, Boerne Church of Christ, 1 Upper Balcones Rd).
  • Boos and Booze at Lost Oak Cellars (7:00 PM - 10:00 PM, Lost Oak Cellars, 111 Rosewood Ave).
  • Halloween Party at Fair Oaks Roost (8:00 PM - 11:00 PM, Fair Oaks Roost, 9091 Fair Oaks Pkwy, Fair Oaks Ranch).
  • Champion High School Homecoming Football Game (7:00 PM, Champion High School, against McCallum).

Saturday, October 31

  • Boerne Cemetery Tour. Ten local community members/actors will be on hand to tell about the lives of the residents who came before us at Boerne Cemetery, 700 N. School St. Tours offered at 10:15 a.m., 10:45 a.m., and 11:15 a.m. Free admission.
  • Boerne Ghost Tours. Explore the darker side of Boerne's history during a 90-minute guided walking tour hosted by DeVino Cellars. The evening begins at 7:30 p.m. at DeVino, 112 E. San Antonio Ave. Tickets begin at $28.52.
  • Cibolo Farmers Market at Herff Farm (9:00 AM - 1:00 PM CDT, Herff Farm).
  • FUMC Halloween Festival. The community is invited from 5:30-7 p.m. to enjoy trunk-or-treating, inflatables, games, activities for all ages, and a free dinner at First United Methodist Church, 205 James St.
  • German Motor Gathering. From 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., come by Boerne Main Plaza to celebrate automotive craftsmanship at the German Motor Gathering, a Hill Country concours-style event showcasing German marques. Cars, motorcycles, and scooters are invited to preregister, while spectators can enjoy the display free of charge along with food and music.
  • Hill Country Rib Night (5:00 PM - 9:00 PM CDT).
  • Honey Creek Guided Walk - at Guadalupe River State Park (9:00 AM - 11:30 AM CDT).
  • NAMIWalks Kendall County. NAMIWalks is America's fundraising event that is a celebration of individuals, families, and communities coming together to support Mental Health for All. The walk will begin at 8 a.m. at Patrick Heath Public Library. Contact: Ernest Espinoza, ernest.e@nami-sat.org.
  • Singer/Songwriter Live Music on the Patio @ Tapatio Springs (12:00 AM - 11:59 PM CDT).
  • Trunks, Treats, and Fire Trucks! (9:00 AM - 8:00 PM, Nissan of Boerne, 31805 I-10 W, with a visit from the Boerne Fire Department from 12 PM to 2 PM).
  • Boerne's Trick or Treat Trail at Hill Country Mile (10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Hill Country Mile, 100 North Main St).
  • Javelina HD Fall Freedom Fest and Trunk or Treat (11:00 AM - 3:00 PM, Javelina Harley-Davidson, 29078 Interstate 10 Frontage Rd).
  • Boerne Ghost Stories at Kuhlmann-King Historical Complex (12:00 PM - 5:00 PM, Kuhlmann-King Historical Complex, 402 East Blanco Road).
  • Trunk or Treat at Comfort Baptist Church (5:00 PM - 8:00 PM, Comfort Baptist Church, 100 Amber Drive, Comfort).
  • Arena Polo Tournament (October 31 to November 1, Spanish Polo Club, free admission).

Sunday, November 1

  • DeVino Ladies Night (4:00 PM - 9:00 PM CDT).
  • Passport Possibilities Private Business Getaway hosted by Vacay Vibes Travel by Shonda (7:00 PM - 8:00 PM CDT, Das Greenhaus).
  • Ribbon Cutting for Little to Luxe (1:30 PM - 2:00 PM CDT).
  • Trivia Night at Tapatio Springs (6:30 PM - 8:30 PM CDT, La Cascada Table & Bar - Patio).

Friday night is also football night: check The Kendall Wire's Friday Night Finals roundup for Boerne High and Champion High scores.
