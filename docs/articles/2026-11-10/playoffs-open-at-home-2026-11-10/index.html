Boerne and Champion open the playoffs at home

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 10, 2026

Boerne High opens the football playoffs at home Thursday night, hosting San Antonio Brackenridge at 7 p.m. at Boerne ISD Stadium.

The Greyhounds enter the first round of the playoffs, the bi-district round, at 10-0. It is only the second perfect regular season in program history; the other was 2022, when Boerne advanced to the state championship game. Boerne is ranked No. 6 in the Beard poll. The winner faces Mission Veterans Memorial or Mercedes in the area round.

Boerne Champion opens its playoff run at home Friday night, hosting Eagle Pass Winn at 7 p.m. at Boerne ISD Stadium.

The Chargers finished the regular season 9-1 and second in their district. The game is the Class 5A Division I bi-district round, while Boerne plays in the Class 5A Division II bracket.

What happens next: the winners advance to the area round next week. The Wire will report the finals Friday night.
