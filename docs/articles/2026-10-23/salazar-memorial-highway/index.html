Court to vote on Salazar memorial highway marker

Kendall County Commissioners Court will consider a highway sign agreement Tuesday that would designate the Officer Russell "Rusty" Salazar Memorial Highway. The agreement covers US 87 in Kendall County, from the Kerr County line to Interstate 10.

The item, listed as a named-marker or memorial-marker highway sign agreement, comes from County Judge Shane Stolarczyk. The court takes it up at its regular meeting Tuesday, October 27, at 9 a.m.

Salazar served the Kendall County Community Supervision and Corrections Department for more than 11 years. He died in the line of duty on January 31, 2019, in a single-vehicle crash at the interchange of I-10 and US 87 in Kendall County, according to the Boerne Star and the 100 Club of Kendall County. He was returning to his office after conducting a field visit with a high-risk sex offender when, during heavy rainfall with low visibility, his vehicle left the road and overturned. He was survived by his wife and five children.

Earlier this year, the court approved resolutions requesting memorial markers for Salazar and Kendall County sheriff's Deputy Carlos "Smiley" Ramirez on Interstate 10 East, with the Texas Department of Transportation to act next, per the Boerne Star. The Tuesday agreement would put Salazar's name on US 87.
