Boerne holds first NAMIWalks along the Old No. 9 Trail

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 6, 2026

Boerne held its first NAMIWalks on October 31, a one-mile walk for mental health awareness that started at the Patrick Heath Public Library amphitheater and followed the Old No. 9 Trail.

Lauren Byrd, executive director of NAMI Greater San Antonio, praised the turnout for the first walk in Boerne. Organizers said one in four adults and one in seven children have a mental health diagnosis.

Joining Byrd on stage before the walk were Boerne Police Chief Steve Perez, Kendall County Sheriff Al Auxier, and Hill Country Family Services Executive Director Bryce Boddie.

Perez said his officers and Auxier's deputies intervene when conditions are at their worst, and both agencies have staff trained to respond to mental health emergencies. He said the challenge is getting people who need help in touch with the people who can give it, and he noted the toll on families.

Byrd pointed residents to the NAMI Bexar Warm Line at 210-939-9999, a free pre-crisis line staffed by trained peers, not an emergency hotline. "Asking for help isn't a sign of weakness; it's a sign of strength," Byrd said.

What happens next: the warm line is open for anyone with questions or concerns for a friend, a family member, or themselves. In an emergency, call 911.
