Boerne ISD superintendent named Superintendent of the Year

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 6, 2026

Boerne ISD Superintendent Kristin Craft will receive the Lone Star State School Counselor Association's Superintendent of the Year award on November 15 in Dallas.

Craft will be recognized during the association's 2026 Annual Conference at the Lone Star Awards Gala. The association said it is recognizing her leadership and commitment to strengthening school counseling programs and supporting student success.

Under Craft, the district established a Student Support Services Team to work with its Safety and Security Team as part of a coordinated system of care, so students receive physical, emotional, and social support. Efforts credited to Craft include expanding early college counseling services at the high school level and strengthening support for school counselors through coaching, program development, and coordinated crisis responses.

"What truly sets Dr. Craft apart is her belief that student success depends on supporting the whole child," said Rachel Franco, the district's counseling coordinator.

What happens next: Craft receives the Professional Recognition Award, Superintendent of the Year, at the Lone Star Awards Gala on Sunday, November 15, in Dallas.
