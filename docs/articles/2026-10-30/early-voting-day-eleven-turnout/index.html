Early Voting Day Eleven: Thursday Draws 1,277 Voters

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 30, 2026

Day eleven of early voting is in the books for Kendall County. Thursday, October 29 drew 1,277 in-person voters, down 94 from Wednesday's 1,371.

Wednesday, October 28 drew 1,371 in-person voters, down 157 from Tuesday's 1,528.

The 11-day in-person total is 17,262, which the county puts at 41.79 percent turnout. The county's in-person table uses 41,302 registered voters; the county elections page lists 41,263.

Mail-ballot totals moved as well. Accepted mail ballots now total 980, up 132 from the prior total of 848. The county reports 1,363 mail ballots sent, 1,005 received, 15 pending, and 7 undeliverable. The county's October 29 row shows 133 mail ballots received.

Combined in-person plus accepted mail ballots: 18,242, about 44.2 percent of the 41,263 registered voters listed on the county elections page.

A same-day comparison to November 2025 early voting was not included because an equivalent-day figure could not be verified from a county or Secretary of State source.

Early voting ends Friday, October 30, at the Kendall County Courthouse Annex, 221 Fawn Valley Drive in Boerne, with polls open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.

What happens next: Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, with polls open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.
