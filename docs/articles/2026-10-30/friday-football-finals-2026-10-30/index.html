Greyhounds and Chargers Both Win: Friday Night Finals, October 30

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 30, 2026

Both Boerne ISD varsity football teams were in action on Friday, October 30, and both came away with wins.

Boerne Greyhounds 28, New Braunfels 17

The Boerne Greyhounds beat New Braunfels 28-17 on the road in conference play on Friday, October 30. The win moves the Greyhounds to 9-0 on the season.

Champion Chargers 61, McCallum 0

The Champion Chargers beat McCallum 61-0 at home in conference play on Friday, October 30. The win moves the Chargers to 8-1 on the season.

Next up

Boerne Greyhounds hosts Legacy Ranch on Friday, November 6; Champion Chargers travels to Manor on Friday, November 6. Kickoff is set for 7 p.m.
