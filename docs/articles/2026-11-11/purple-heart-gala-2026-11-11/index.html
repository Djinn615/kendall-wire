Purple Heart Project gala honors fallen soldier, raises 80,000 dollars

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 11, 2026

The Purple Heart Project held its seventh annual gala Saturday at The Kendall, honoring Army Pfc. Isabella Gonzales, a 19-year-old from Carrollton killed in Jordan in July, and four local Purple Heart recipients. The auction raised 80,000 dollars.

Gonzales was killed on July 17 during an enemy attack at Muwaffaq Salti Air Base in Jordan. She was assigned to the 1st Battalion, 57th Air Defense Artillery Regiment at U.S. Army Garrison Ansbach in Germany, and was supporting Operation Inherent Resolve. She had graduated from Hebron High School in 2025.

Navy veteran and Gold Star father Tony Ruiz presented Gonzales's story at the gala. His son, Army Sgt. Clinton Keith Ruiz, was killed in action in Afghanistan on Oct. 25, 2012.

"She had her whole life ahead of her, but she made the decision, like so many of us in this room, to raise her right hand and take an oath to serve our country," Ruiz said.

The event also honored Marine Cpl. William Fluitt, Army Lt. Col. Frank Trevino, Army Lt. Col. Steve Spence and Army Sgt. 1st Class Scott Adams. Each veteran received a custom belt buckle, a Purple Heart commemorative coin and a Turtlebox outdoor Bluetooth speaker.

Of the auction proceeds, 5,000 dollars went to the Boerne High School culinary class and 4,000 dollars went to a local charity. H-E-B provided 10,000 dollars for the event, and beer distributor Keg 1 O'Neal LLC is a longtime supporter.

The Boerne-Champion High School Air Force Junior Reserve Officer Training Corps posted the colors, and the Boerne High School culinary class served the entrees.

The Purple Heart Project was founded by local chef and Army veteran Mark Sierra. The project began in 2020 with a steak dinner at his restaurant, Compadres Hill Country Cocina, for National Purple Heart Day, according to the organization's website.

"Tonight we are honoring those Purple Heart recipients that paid the ultimate sacrifice," Sierra said.
