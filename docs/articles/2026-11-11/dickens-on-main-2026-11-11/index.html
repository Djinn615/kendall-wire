Dickens on Main returns November 27 and 28

Reported by The Kendall Wire • November 11, 2026

Dickens on Main returns to downtown Boerne on Friday, November 27, and Saturday, November 28, bringing a Victorian Christmas celebration to the Hill Country Mile.

The two-day event draws nearly 50,000 visitors a year from across Texas. It opens the holiday season in Boerne, which draws more than 100,000 guests across its holiday events.

The Christmas market and food vendors will be open from noon to 9 p.m. both days. Children's inflatables run from 3 to 9 p.m., and the snow slide runs from 6 to 9 p.m. each evening.

Live entertainment runs through both days, with performances by the Caleb Young Band, Marcy Grace Band, Guy Vincent and SuperSquad. Visitors can also see scenes from "The Nutcracker," birds of prey demonstrations by Last Chance Forever, and ice carving shows that end in an ice carving battle each night.

Friday evening at the Main Plaza gazebo, a Christmas carol sing-along hosted by Boerne Bible Church and a performance by Castle Brass lead into the tree lighting ceremony at 6 p.m., with the official lighting at 6:30 p.m.

Main Street between River Road and Blanco Road will be closed to vehicle traffic during the event. All vehicles must be removed from that stretch by 2:30 p.m. Friday and Saturday. Vehicles left after that time may be towed at the owner's expense.

Public parking will be available on a first-come, first-served basis at City Hall and the Patrick Heath Public Library, along with street parking in surrounding areas. Parking at the Boerne Visitor Center will be limited to guests who need accessible parking.

What happens next: the holiday season continues the following weekend with the annual Weihnachts Parade on Saturday, December 5. The full schedule is at HolidaysInBoerne.com.
