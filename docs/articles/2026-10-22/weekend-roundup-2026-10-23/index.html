This Weekend in Boerne and Kendall County: October 23-25

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 22, 2026

Here is what is on around Boerne and Kendall County from Friday, October 23 to Sunday, October 25.

Friday, October 23

  • 5th Annual Boerne Chamber Pickleball Tournament presented by Alliance Credit Union (12:00 PM - 5:00 PM CDT, Fair Oaks Ranch Golf & Country Club).
  • Singer/Songwriter Live Music on the Patio @ Tapatio Springs (12:00 AM - 11:59 PM CDT).
  • Friday at the Patrick Heath Public Library (451 N. Main St.): Play & Learn Workshop (10:00 AM - 11:00 AM CDT), Public Memory Lab Open (10:00 AM - 2:00 PM CDT), Free-to-Play Fridays (4:00 PM - 6:00 PM CDT), and the Unidos Somos Más exhibit on view (9:00 AM - 7:00 PM CDT).
  • Trunk or Treat at The Hacienda (5:30 PM - 7:30 PM CDT, 601 Esperanza Blvd).
  • Hill Country Rib Night at La Cascada Table & Bar (5:00 PM - 9:00 PM CDT, 1 Resort Way). Baby back ribs dinner special.
  • Haunted at the Hospital at Camp Bar and Feed (421 S Main St). Runs through October 31.

Saturday, October 24

  • 2026 Boots and Blessings Gala Online Auction & Raffle Supporting The Hope Center Food Pantry and Outreach.
  • 38th Annual South Texas All British Car Day (8:30 AM - 2:30 PM CDT, Main Plaza). British automobiles on display; judging ends at noon.
  • Cibolo Farmers Market at Herff Farm (9:00 AM - 1:00 PM CDT, HERFF FARM).
  • Butterfly Day (9:00 AM - 1:00 PM CDT, 33 Herff Road). Family-friendly activities during the farmers market, including butterfly tagging, temporary tattoos and native wildflower seedballs.
  • Community Rag Rug Making at the Kuhlmann-King House (12:30 PM - 2:00 PM CDT, Kuhlmann-King Historic House Museum).
  • Early Learning Center of St Mark Presbyterian Church's Fall Fest (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM CDT, St Mark Presbyterian Church, 208 W. Bandera). Family morning with bounce houses, a petting zoo, face painting and trunk or treat.
  • Honey Creek Guided Walk - at Guadalupe River State Park (9:00 AM - 11:30 AM CDT).
  • Early Settlers of the Honey Creek Area: Homestead Site Visit (12:30 PM - 2:30 PM CDT, Honey Creek State Natural Area).
  • Kuhlmann-King Historical Complex Free Tours (12:00 PM - 3:00 PM CDT, Kuhlmann-King Historical Complex).
  • Singer/Songwriter Live Music on the Patio @ Tapatio Springs (12:00 AM - 11:59 PM CDT).
  • Harvest Time Antique Tractor Pull (10:00 AM - 4:00 PM CDT, The AgriCultural Museum & Arts Center, 102 City Park Rd.). Free. Vintage tractor competition, food trucks and family fun.
  • Bluegrass Jam (11:00 AM - 1:00 PM CDT, The AgriCultural, 102 City Park Road). Bring an instrument and play along or just listen. Free.
  • Julia Rose at Singing Water Vineyards (1:00 PM - 4:00 PM CDT, Singing Water Vineyards, 316 Mill Dam Rd, Comfort). Live country and classic rock.
  • Boerne Boo (4:00 PM - 7:00 PM CDT, Patrick Heath Public Library, 451 N. Main St.). Free. Trick-or-treating, bounce houses, carnival games, food trucks, music, candy and a costume contest.
  • Trunk or Treat at Texas Roadhouse Leon Springs (11:00 AM - 2:00 PM CDT). Fall Market combined with trunk or treat.
  • Live Music at The Dodging Duck Brewhaus (5:30 PM - 8:30 PM CDT, The Dodging Duck Brewhaus, Boerne).
  • Hill Country Rib Night at La Cascada Table & Bar (5:00 PM - 9:00 PM CDT, 1 Resort Way). Baby back ribs dinner special.
  • Trunk-or-Treat at Van Raub Elementary (from 6:00 PM CDT, 8776 Dietz Elkhorn Rd, Fair Oaks Ranch).
  • Rock, Boo's, Blue's & Brews (6:00 PM - 10:30 PM CDT, Paradise Live, 25389 Boerne Stage Rd, San Antonio). Tickets 10 USD.
  • The Bistro Boos & Brews Bash (7:30 PM - 10:30 PM CDT, 259 Brantley's Bistro & Bar, 259 South Main St, Boerne).
  • Boerne Ghost Tours at DeVino Cellars (8:30 PM - 10:00 PM CDT, DeVino Cellars, 1109 S Main St, Boerne).

Sunday, October 25

  • Halloween Fall Festival and Trunk or Treat at St. John Lutheran Church (12:00 PM - 1:45 PM CDT).
  • Honey Creek Guided Walk - at Guadalupe River State Park (9:00 AM - 11:30 AM CDT).

Friday night is also football night: check The Kendall Wire's Friday Night Finals roundup for Boerne High and Champion High scores.
