Day Six of Early Voting Adds 1,015 In-Person Voters in Kendall County

Reported by The Kendall Wire • October 25, 2026

Kendall County posted its sixth day of early voting numbers for the Nov 3, 2026 joint election, and 1,015 people voted in person on Saturday, Oct 24.

Saturday was the lightest day of early voting so far, down 919 from Friday's 1,934. The six-day in-person total stands at 11,052, which the county reports as 26.76 percent turnout.

The daily totals so far: 2,165 on Monday, Oct 19; 2,148 on Tuesday, Oct 20; 1,972 on Wednesday, Oct 21; 1,818 on Thursday, Oct 22; 1,934 on Friday, Oct 23; and 1,015 on Saturday, Oct 24.

The county's turnout table uses 41,302 registered voters for its percentage. The county elections page separately lists 41,263 registered voters as of October 2026.

On the mail-ballot side, the county's by-mail roster, updated through Oct 24, shows 1,363 ballots sent to voters and 803 returned, with 781 accepted, 14 pending voter action to correct a defect on the carrier envelope, and 5 undeliverable. Saturday added 66 returned ballots, 65 accepted and 1 pending.

Combined, in-person votes plus accepted mail ballots total 11,833, about 28.7 percent of the county's registered voters.

The Wire looked for a same-day comparison from the Nov 4, 2025 election, whose sixth day of early voting fell on Saturday, Oct 25, 2025. No daily Kendall County early voting figures for that election could be verified from a public source: the county's posted records for 2025 list final results only, and the Secretary of State's site carries no daily county-level numbers or downloadable data files for that election. The Wire will run the year-over-year comparison when a verifiable figure is available.

Early voting continues at the Kendall County Courthouse Annex, 221 Fawn Valley Drive in Boerne. Hours: 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. today, Sunday, Oct 25; and 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Monday, Oct 26 through Friday, Oct 30. The deadline to apply for a ballot by mail was Friday, Oct 23; the application had to be received by the county, not just postmarked. Election Day is Nov 3, with polls open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.

A reminder on the math: the county's daily tables count voters checked in, and its turnout percentage uses the county's own registered-voter figure. Small rounding differences are normal.
