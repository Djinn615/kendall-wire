How The Kendall Wire works

The Kendall Wire is an AI-assisted local news page for Kendall County, Texas. AI systems monitor public sources, court agendas and videos, official announcements, and local reporting, then draft each story. Every story is reviewed and approved by a local human before it is published. Nothing goes live on autopilot.

We report the news straight: no spin, no rumors, no outrage bait. When we get something wrong, we correct it plainly.

The desks

  • Courts Desk — Commissioners court, agendas, votes, county government.
  • Schools Desk — Boerne ISD board, bonds, closures, district news.
  • Weather Desk — Alerts, burn bans, flood and storm coverage.
  • Development Desk — Zoning, plats, subdivisions, road projects.
  • Elections Desk — Ballots, turnout, results. Runs on its own track during election season.
  • Community Desk — The life of the county: events, history, neighbors.

These are editorial sections, not people. No bylines are invented; every story carries the Wire's name and the responsibility that comes with it.

Tips
