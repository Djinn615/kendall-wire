For the Record

For the Record is The Kendall Wire's weekly podcast. Each episode looks back at the week in Kendall County, and it is built only from stories the Wire reported, verified, and published that week. Nothing is added, and nothing is spun.

The voices are AI-generated, and we say so at the top of every episode. The reporting behind them follows the same rule as everything else on this site: a person reads every word and checks it against the source before it publishes.

Episode 1: The Week of November 9, 2026

The Greyhounds roll in their playoff opener, both Boerne girls teams open basketball season with wins, and three volleyball seasons come to an end. The GVTC Foundation awards grants to 183 nonprofits. Kendall County marks Veterans Day with the Purple Heart Project gala and the ceremony at Veterans Plaza, and the plaza's own bronze turns 34. New World screwworm is found in Bandera County. TxDOT updates residents on Texas 46 West. And the holiday calendar fills up: Dickens on Main and the 40th Christmas in Comfort.
