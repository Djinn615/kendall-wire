## TxDOT: I-10 and Highway 46 corridors

### FM 3351 corridor (I-10 to SH 46)

- For more information on the construction status of this project, please contact the TxDOT San Antonio District Office Public Information Officer for Comal and Kendall counties at 512-658-6177.
- The proposed project would also include the construction of a 10-foot shared-use path on the west side of FM 3351 throughout the project limits.
- Environmental studies and schematic design are in progress for this proposed project.
- 17, 2024 – Virtual Public Meeting and In-person Open House Nov.
- I-10 Eastbound Frontage Road Turnaround Bridge The fourth project is a funded improvement anticipated to be let for construction in 2027.
- Project information Schedules outlining the major milestones of the project development process, including data collection, schematic development, environmental studies, and public involvement, can be found on the proposed project pages hyperlinked above.
- Project updates For construction updates, follow the San Antonio TxDOT District Facebook , X , and Nextdoor social media pages.
- For construction project information, please contact a TxDOT San Antonio Public Information Officer for Comal and Kendall counties at 512-658-6177 and for Bexar County at 210-615-5839.

Project page: https://www.txdot.gov/projects/projects-studies/san-antonio/fm3351-corridor-projects.html

### FM 3351 Desperado Way to SH 46

- Project description TxDOT is proposing to widen FM 3351 within the project limits.

Project page: http://www.txdot.gov/projects/projects-studies/san-antonio/fm3351-corridor-projects/fm3351-from-desperado-way-to-sh46.html

### US 281 and SH 46

- Current construction activities The contractor is currently performing nighttime paving operations for the final surface of SH 46.
- Traffic is anticipated to be shifted into its final configuration in June.
- Additional work remains, including installation of traffic signals and completion of roadside elements.

Project page: http://www.txdot.gov/projects/projects-studies/san-antonio/us281-sh46.html

SH 46 West (Boerne to Bandera) safety work is tracked through the county-posted TxDOT planning presentation and local reporting; TxDOT does not maintain a dedicated project page for that segment.
