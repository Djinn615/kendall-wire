The annual Gone But Not Forgotten Tour returns to Boerne Cemetery on October 31, offering residents a walk through the county's history told by the people who lived it.

Sponsored by the Genealogical Society of Kendall County, the Patrick Heath Public Library, and the City of Boerne Parks and Recreation Department, the tour features actors in period dress portraying eight former Boerne residents at their final resting places.

This year's portrayals: Dr. William G. Kingsbury (1823-1896), Carolyn Kampermann Herff (1904-1922), William George Hughes (1859-1902), Emil Richter (1858-1920), Edith Agnes Gray (1898-1975), Alice Krause Gerfers (1918-2014), Ferdinand Harz (1824-1903), and Rudolph Carstanjen (1827-1902).

Organizers emphasize this is not a ghost tour. The presentations are historically accurate, respectful tributes honoring the lives and contributions of those buried in the cemetery.

Walking tours begin at the cemetery's east gate on School Street at 10:15, 10:45, and 11:15 a.m.
