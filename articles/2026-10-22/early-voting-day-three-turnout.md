Kendall County posted its third day of early voting numbers for the Nov 3, 2026 joint election, and 1,972 people voted in person on Wednesday, Oct 21.

The county's daily in-person report lists 1,964 main early voters plus 8 limited-ballot voters, for a 1,972 total. That is down 176 from Tuesday's 2,148 and 193 from Monday's 2,165, the lightest turnout of the three days so far. The three-day in-person total stands at 6,285, which the county reports as 15.22 percent turnout. No provisional ballots have been cast during early voting.

The county's turnout table uses 41,302 registered voters for its percentage. The county elections page separately lists 41,263 registered voters as of October 2026, a difference of 39 voters that does not move the percentage.

On the mail-ballot side, 88 ballots were returned on Wednesday. The county's by-mail roster, updated through Oct 21, shows 1,268 ballots sent to voters and 633 returned, with 614 accepted, 11 pending voter action to correct a defect on the carrier envelope, and 5 undeliverable.

Combined, in-person votes plus accepted mail ballots total 6,899, roughly 16.7 percent of the county's registered voters.

The Wire looked for a same-day comparison from the Nov 4, 2025 election, whose third day of early voting fell on Wednesday, Oct 22, 2025. No daily Kendall County early voting figures for that election could be verified from a public source: the county's posted records for 2025 list final results only, and the Secretary of State's site carries no daily county-level numbers or downloadable data files for that election. The Wire will run the year-over-year comparison when a verifiable figure is available.

Early voting continues at the Kendall County Courthouse Annex, 221 Fawn Valley Drive in Boerne. Hours: 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. today and Friday; 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Saturday, Oct 24; 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. Sunday, Oct 25; and 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Monday, Oct 26 through Friday, Oct 30. The last day to apply for a ballot by mail is Friday, Oct 23; the application must be received by the county, not just postmarked. Election Day is Nov 3, with polls open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.

A reminder on the math: the county's daily tables count voters checked in, and its turnout percentage uses the county's own registered-voter figure. Small rounding differences are normal.
